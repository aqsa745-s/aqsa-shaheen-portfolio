## Selected Projects

### 1. Inventory Risk Classification using Machine Learning
**Status:** Academic decision-support prototype

An academic prototype for profiling retail product demand, exploring product groups with K-Means, assigning transparent inventory-risk screening levels and estimating basic replenishment quantities.

- Cleans retail transaction data and aggregates monthly product demand.
- Calculates Average Demand Interval (ADI) and squared coefficient of variation (CV²).
- Uses K-Means on transformed demand indicators as an exploratory unsupervised learning step.
- Calculates a separate transparent 0–100 rule-based risk score and Low / Medium / High screening bands.
- Demonstrates Moving Average, Croston SBA and ARIMA forecasting, plus basic safety-stock and reorder-point scenarios.
- Provides a Streamlit dashboard and downloadable reports.

**Method note:** K-Means is the machine-learning component. Risk bands come from a project-defined scoring rule, not a supervised classifier trained on stockout labels. The sample dataset is for educational demonstration and is not live company inventory data.

- **Repository:** https://github.com/aqsa745-s/Inventory-Risk-Classification-ML
