A SHORT GUIDE

How to use the tools

You do not need to know every formula to try these tools. Follow these three steps and take time to understand what the result means.

01 / ENTER

Add your information

Use the sample numbers or enter your own sales and supplier details. Check that the numbers are correct.

02 / TRY

Run the tool

Press the calculate or compare button. Change a number or a weight and see how the result changes.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Check the result

Read the explanation. Think about whether the result makes sense for the business before making a decision.

Please note: These are learning tools, not professional business systems. The demand tool uses simple forecasting methods and does not include seasons, promotions or sudden changes. The supplier tool depends on the information and importance levels entered by the user. Always check real business information before acting.
