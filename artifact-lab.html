ARTIFACT 02 / SUPPLY-CHAIN DECISION SCIENCETOPSIS

Supplier Comparison & Ranking

Method: TOPSIS normalizes each criterion column, multiplies it by its normalized weight, identifies the ideal best and worst values, calculates each supplier's Euclidean distance from both ideals, and computes a closeness score between 0 and 1. A higher score indicates a closer match to the weighted ideal among the suppliers entered. Rankings depend on the selected criteria, data quality and weights.

HOW TO USE THESE TOOLS

Good decisions start with good assumptions.

01 / INPUT

Use relevant data

Enter consistent historical demand or comparable supplier measurements. Check units, ranges and missing information.

02 / MODEL

Inspect the method

Understand how forecast errors or TOPSIS scores are calculated. Change assumptions and observe how the outcome changes.

03 / VALIDATE

Review before acting

Compare outputs with business context, additional evidence and expert judgement. A model ranking is decision support, not an automatic purchase instruction.

Limitations: These are educational prototypes, not production forecasting or procurement systems. Forecasting methods are deliberately introductory and do not model seasonality, promotions, stock-outs or external drivers. Supplier rankings depend on the entered criteria and weights and do not replace supplier due diligence.
