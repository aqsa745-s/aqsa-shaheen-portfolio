HOW TO READ THE OUTPUT

Every number needs context.

01 / INPUTS

Check assumptions

Use realistic units and a relevant time period. A daily demand estimate should be based on suitable historical data.

02 / MODEL

Understand the formula

The calculator applies a transparent formula. It does not learn from data or guarantee a future outcome.

03 / DECISION

Review before acting

Consider supplier delays, pending purchase orders, seasonal demand, taxes, discounts and other costs before making a business decision.

Limitations: These tools are learning prototypes, not production systems or professional financial advice. Inventory calculations assume steady average demand and do not account for existing purchase orders, variable lead times or seasonality. Profitability estimates exclude taxes, returns, overhead allocation and other costs unless entered as fixed costs.
