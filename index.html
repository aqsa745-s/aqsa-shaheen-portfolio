Inventory Risk Classification using Machine Learning

An academic decision-support prototype that analyses retail product demand patterns, explores product groups with K-Means and applies a transparent rule-based inventory-risk score. It also demonstrates demand forecasting and basic replenishment calculations through a Streamlit dashboard.
